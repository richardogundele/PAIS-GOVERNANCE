# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

PAIS-Governance is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for all contributors and users, regardless of:
- Age, race, ethnicity, nationality, or national origin
- Gender identity or expression
- Sexual orientation
- Disability or health status
- Religion or belief
- Socioeconomic status
- Appearance
- Experience level in technology

## Our Standards

### Behavior We Encourage

- **Be respectful** — Treat others with kindness and courtesy
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- **Be clear** — Communicate your ideas thoughtfully
- **Be collaborative** — Work together toward solutions
- **Be professional** — Keep discussions focused on the project

### Behavior We Will Not Tolerate

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive comments or slurs (whether intentional or not)
- Personal attacks or insults
- Unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Disrupting project discussions with off-topic, spammy, or inflammatory content
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Document it** — Note what happened, when, who was involved, and context
2. **Report to the maintainer** — Use the contact route on Richard Ogundele's GitHub profile and state that the report concerns the PAIS code of conduct.
3. **Keep it private** — Do not publicly call out individuals

Include:
- What happened?
- Who was involved?
- When did this occur?
- How did this affect you?
- What would resolution look like?

## Response & Enforcement

The maintainers will:
- Acknowledge your report within 48 hours
- Investigate the issue fairly and impartially
- Keep your identity private (if requested)
- Take appropriate action, which may include:
  - Asking the person to apologize
  - Temporary suspension from the project
  - Permanent ban from the community (in severe cases)

We will not tolerate retaliation against those who report violations in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Slack channel (if applicable)
- Code comments and documentation
- Community events and meetings
- Private communications related to the project
